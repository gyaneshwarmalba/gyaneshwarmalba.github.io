AI Legal Contract Analyzer

Governed RAG pipeline for legal documents

Ingests contracts, chunks and embeds them, retrieves the clauses that matter, and passes answers through a governed LLM layer before anything reaches a reviewer.

View on GitHub

The problem

Legal teams spend a lot of time reading long agreements to find specific clauses, spot risky language, and check terms against policy. Generic chat tools are a poor fit: answers need to cite the source text, stay inside approved models and data boundaries, and leave the final call to a human reviewer.

Architecture

  1. SourcesContract documents from storage or a contract lifecycle system
  2. Ingest and chunkSplit documents into clause-aware chunks with metadata
  3. Vector storeEmbed chunks and index them for similarity search
  4. RAG with AWS BedrockRetrieve relevant clauses and generate grounded, cited answers
  5. ReviewSurface results in Salesforce or a review UI for a human decision

Key design decisions

  • Human in the loop by default. The system suggests and cites; a reviewer approves. That keeps accountability with legal staff.
  • Governed model access. Generation goes through AWS Bedrock so model choice, data residency, and logging follow enterprise controls.
  • Answers grounded in source text. Every answer links back to the retrieved clauses so reviewers can verify it quickly.
  • Fits existing workflows. Results are designed to land where legal teams already work (Salesforce) instead of in a separate tool.

Tech stack
